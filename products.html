Welcome to Our Menu

At Aroma Coffee & Tea House, we believe every cup should deliver a memorable experience. Our selection includes carefully prepared coffee and refreshing tea to suit different tastes and preferences. Whether you prefer a strong coffee or a light cup of tea, we have something for you to enjoy.

1. Our Coffee Selection

Our coffee is prepared to provide rich aromas and satisfying flavours. Explore some of our popular coffee varieties below.

Espresso

Espresso is a concentrated coffee drink made by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee beans. It has a strong flavour and is served in a small cup. It is also used as the base for several other coffee beverages.

Cappuccino

Cappuccino is a popular coffee beverage made with espresso, steamed milk and milk foam. It has a creamy texture and a balanced coffee flavour, making it a favourite among many coffee lovers.

Americano

Americano is prepared by adding hot water to espresso. It has a lighter consistency than espresso while maintaining a rich coffee taste. It is suitable for customers who prefer a simple black coffee.

2. Our Tea Selection

We also offer a variety of teas with different flavours and aromas. Our tea selection is ideal for customers looking for a refreshing or relaxing beverage.

Green Tea

Green tea is made from tea leaves that undergo minimal oxidation during processing. It has a light, refreshing flavour and can be served plain or with honey according to preference.

Black Tea

Black tea has a rich flavour and a darker colour. It can be enjoyed plain or served with milk, sugar or lemon. It is a popular choice for breakfast and afternoon refreshments.

Herbal Tea

Herbal tea is prepared by steeping herbs, flowers or other plant materials in hot water. Depending on the ingredients used, it can have floral, fruity or refreshing flavours.
